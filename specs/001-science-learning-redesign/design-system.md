# Science Field Journal: design inventory

Concept references: `concepts/entry.png`, `library.png`, `preview-error.png`, `practice.png`, `results.png`, `adult.png`, and `mobile.png`. These are directional design studies, not a source of real deck content. Actual product data and behavior take precedence. Reject decorative or invented labels, fake scientific diagrams, unsupported account and navigation features, automatic grade filtering, and arbitrary sample scores. The concepts do not establish accessibility compliance.

- **Visual direction:** a naturalist's field journal: warm paper, high-contrast ink, dark teal action and disciplined science illustration. Keep generous readable space; simplify busy borders and illustrations on small screens. Use real authored deck titles/questions only.
- **Approximate tokens to verify:** paper `#f8f1e5`; ink `#172c2a`; teal `#096961`; dark teal `#163c38`; border `#c6b9a4`; focus `#136bd0`; feedback green `#225d42` and rust `#923c2f` against light surfaces. Test all actual foreground/background pairs, including dark theme.
- **Type:** expressive readable serif for major headings and questions; humanist system sans for control labels, instructions, long data and status. Scale headings with viewport, preserve at least 16px controls and line heights, avoid tiny handwritten text as information.
- **Layout:** compact session bar; centered content shell with generous margins; clear single column on phone; discoverable list rows with title, description, count and action. Present one question at a time with a stable feedback region and a clear results review.
- **Components:** consistent primary filled teal button, secondary outline button, large text input, purposeful list rows, disclosure panels and explicit save status. Provide visible hover, keyboard focus, disabled and error states, touch targets and labels; no control may be decorative.
- **Images:** sparse naturalist illustrations for atmosphere, never imply a generated diagram or unrelated example accurately depicts an actual quiz question. Production illustration assets need separate creation and review. Do not reproduce the desktop-window chrome or invented navigation from the concepts.
- **Responsive and motion:** stack actions and rows at narrow widths, accommodate long terms and 200% zoom, keep focus visible, and avoid forced animation. Respect reduced-motion and do not rely on color, sound or movement to communicate correctness.
- **Content guardrails:** guest nickname is public when points are earned; managed profiles default private; child sign-in/accounts do not exist. Missing explanations and subject metadata remain missing/uncategorized. No fake resume, personalized mastery, classes or learner data.
